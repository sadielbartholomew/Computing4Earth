## Families of models

A **model family** is a collection of related numerical models that share a common codebase,
scientific heritage and/or development team. Over time, successive versions of the family
incorporate new physical parameterisations, numerical methods, resolutions and
component models.

An example is the
[HadGEM (Hadley Centre Global Environment Model)](https://www.metoffice.gov.uk/research/approach/modelling-systems/unified-model/climate-models/hadgem3)
family developed mainly the UK's Met Office Hadley Centre.

The HadGEM family illustrates how climate models evolve over time. While successive generations
generally incorporate improvements in spatial resolution, scientific development has
also focused on enhancing the representation of physical, chemical and biological processes.
Modern families such as HadGEM3 therefore comprise multiple scientific configurations and
resolutions, each designed for different applications ranging from operational NWP to
global climate projections (TODO cite Roberts 2019).
